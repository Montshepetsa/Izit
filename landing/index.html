How a night works

01 Name the room. Pair 1 with 2, 3 with 4.
02 Guesser holds the phone to their forehead, screen out.
03 Only the partner clues. No saying the word.
04 Tilt down for correct. Tilt up to skip.
05 Sixty seconds, then pass inside the pair.
06 Everyone guesses once. Highest pair total wins.
